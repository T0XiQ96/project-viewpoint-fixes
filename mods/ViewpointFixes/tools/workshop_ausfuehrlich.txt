title=Project Viewpoint - Fixes
description=[h1]Project Viewpoint - Fixes[/h1]
description=Fifteen small, independent mods for [b]Project Viewpoint[/b] (3D view), Build 42.21. Enable only the ones you want. Mods 1-14 are [b]client-side[/b]; mod 12 changes where your own shots go. Mod 15 (Vehicle Ballistics & Penetration) changes gameplay and is needed on the server too. All need ZombieBuddy and go [b]after Viewpoint[/b]. Java source is in each mod's source/ folder. Unofficial, not affiliated with the authors of Viewpoint or the patched mods.
description=
description=[h2]Map markers and labels[/h2]
description=[b]1) Viewpoint 2D to 3D[/b] (Viewpoint2Dto3D)
description=Many mods place hints with the old isometric projection (Lua isoToScreenX/Y), so under Viewpoint they float at the wrong spot.
description=[list]
description=[*] While the 3D view is on, isoToScreenX/Y return the real screen position from Viewpoint's camera (first person, third person, free camera). Points behind the camera are moved far off screen so mods hide them.
description=[*] Switches off the flat 2D vehicle smoke of Real Vehicle VFX (engine exhaust, damage smoke, radiator steam, tire smoke) while the 3D view is on; your settings come back afterwards.
description=[*] 3D off or Viewpoint missing: exactly vanilla. Markers can lag one frame.
description=[/list]
description=[b]2) Viewpoint 2D to 3D - Extended Patch[/b] (Viewpoint2Dto3D_ExtendedPatch)
description=Needs mod 1. The same for mods using IsoUtils.XToScreen/YToScreen. Checked: Leave A Message Note, Shotgun Trajectory, Home Inventory, Cold Breath, Hide Anywhere. Not covered: renderPoly (e.g. OffGrid) or own Java.
description=
description=[h2]Looks[/h2]
description=[b]3) Open All Containers Fix[/b] (ViewpointOpenContainers)
description=Open All Containers swaps a container for an "open" sprite that Viewpoint has no 3D shape for, so opened containers vanished. Now they use OAC's own geometry, or the closed original's shape if there is none.
description=[b]4) Hide Camera[/b] (ViewpointHideCamera)
description=Hiding under a bed (Hide Anywhere) left the camera at standing eye height above the bed. Now it drops to the floor while hidden (height = one constant in the Lua file).
description=[b]10) 3D Gun Effects, Tracers and Fireflies[/b] (ViewpointEffects3D)
description=Effects that were flat 2D or invisible in 3D, drawn in Viewpoint's scene (hidden behind walls). Each part has an option.
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Tracers:[/b] the game's own bullet tracers in 3D (yours and other players'). Look per ammo type, so weapon mods apply. With Viewpoint True Ballistics every round is drawn where it really flies; no double tracers.
description=[*] [b]Muzzle flash:[/b] a bright flash that lights up the surroundings for a moment, whenever the game itself shows its muzzle flash (depends on weapon and chance, suppressors apply).
description=[*] [b]Gun smoke:[/b] puffs start at the real muzzle and stay in the world; bigger in first person (adjustable). Muzzle Smoke's flat 2D puffs are hidden in 3D.
description=[*] [b]Impacts by material:[/b] the game's own material of the hit tile. Wood: dark hole, splinters. Metal: small hole, sparks. Hardened metal / armoured glass: only a scrape, often ricochets. Stone, concrete, brick: hole, dust, some sparks. Dirt, grass, sand, snow: spray. Ricochets likelier on hard surfaces at flat angles. Size depends on the ammo (mod ammo too). Holes fade after about 2 minutes.
description=[*] [b]Vehicles[/b] (with mod 15): impacts by the part that was really hit - glass, sheet metal, tyre, armour, lamp - with sparks, glass splinters and ricochets. Bullet holes stay on the vehicle and move with it (glass: hole with a crack halo, metal: hole with a bare rim, armour: scrape) until the part is replaced, repaired to 100 % or the window shatters.
description=[*] [b]JB's Fireflies:[/b] drawn in 3D and hidden behind walls, or switched off in 3D (option).
description=[*] The game's impact sound and hits on placed items still happen in 3D. 2D view unchanged.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Controls[/h2]
description=[b]5) Item Placement 3D[/b] (ViewpointPlaceItems)
description=Placing an item (right-click > Place item) showed no preview and did nothing in 3D. Now: 3D preview at the cursor/crosshair on the floor or furniture, [b]R[/b] / [b]Shift+R[/b] rotate, the normal "toggle mode" key changes surface, [b]left click[/b] places, [b]right click[/b] cancels, a hint shows when the spot is invalid. Items without a 3D model show no preview. 2D view unchanged.
description=[b]6) Right Click in Mouse Mode[/b] (ViewpointMouseRightClick)
description=In Viewpoint's mouse mode (free cursor: middle click, loot window, settings, pause) right click started the aim stance. Now it opens the normal context menu. Crosshair view: right click stays aim. If another mod swallows the click, the menu for the square under the mouse opens anyway.
description=[b]7) Crosshair Use[/b] (ViewpointCrosshairUse)
description=Needs mods 6 and 9. [b]Left click[/b] on a light switch you look at switches it like a left click in the normal view (instant when close, no walking). The game itself finds the clicked object with its 2D picture, which in 3D only hit when you happened to stand just right; now the object in the crosshair counts. A [b]short right click[/b] (under 0.25 s) opens the normal right-click menu for exactly the object you look at, with a free mouse pointer; holding stays aim.
description=[b]8) Prone on X[/b] (ViewpointProneKey)
description=Lethal Stealth: [b]X[/b] goes prone, X again gets up (3D view only, not with a firearm in the primary hand because X is "Rack firearm"). The Prone entry is removed from the right-click menu; Lethal Stealth's own key still works.
description=[b]11) Over the Shoulder in 3D[/b] (ViewpointShoulderLook3D)
description=Needs mod 1 and Over the Shoulder. In the 3D view [b]hold the look key[/b] (default [b]Caps Lock[/b], Options > Key Bindings > "Look around in 3D") and move the mouse: the camera looks around while you keep walking where you were going, head and upper body turn and your view widens like Over the Shoulder in 2D. Release: the camera turns back. Aiming ends the look. The middle mouse button stays free for Viewpoint's mouse mode; in the 2D view Over the Shoulder works as before. Replaces the old "No Over the Shoulder in 3D" (ViewpointNoShoulderLook).
description=
description=[h2]Shooting[/h2]
description=[b]12) Aim Spread[/b] (ViewpointAimSpread)
description=Needs [b]Viewpoint True Ballistics[/b] (Workshop 3812864848), loaded after it. Changes gameplay for your own shots.
description=[list]
description=[*] Every shot leaves at a random point inside the game's own aiming circle (the brackets), instead of True Ballistics' own spread. It shrinks with Aiming skill, steady aim and focus and grows with movement and moodles (mods that change it apply). 1.5x its size by default. The round then flies normally with True Ballistics.
description=[*] Only with the sandbox option [b]Firearms Use Damage Chance = Disabled[/b]. With "Zombies only" or "All", shots go exactly to the reticle.
description=[*] Bushes do not stop bullets but can deflect them: 10 % per bush, up to 10 degrees sideways and up/down (both adjustable). The round flies on and hits whatever is on its new path.
description=[*] Options in Options > Mods > Viewpoint Aim Spread.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]13) Crosshair Options[/b] (ViewpointReticle)
description=Viewpoint's centre dot: show always, only with a firearm, with any weapon, only while aiming, or never; dot, cross or ring; size, opacity, colour, outline. The game's aiming brackets can be hidden in 3D too.
description=
description=[b]14) Hold to Zoom[/b] (ViewpointZoomHold)
description=Needs Project Viewpoint QOL. Zoom only while holding a key (default Left Shift) and turning the wheel, in bigger steps; releasing snaps back to the normal view. The plain wheel no longer zooms.
description=
description=[h2]Interaction list[/h2]
description=[b]9) Interaction List Cleanup[/b] (ViewpointInteractList)
description=Viewpoint builds the list next to the crosshair from the game's right-click menu, so mod entries about yourself appeared for every object and even became the heading ("Wellness" over a door). Hidden in Viewpoint's list only (Lifestyle: Wellness/Yoga, dancing, sharing knowledge, cleaning, the admin debug menu; Container Options, Decoholic, Door Bar, Open All Containers auto-close); Hide Anywhere entries move to the top, hint texts like "Missing a Hammer..." no longer pop up. The normal right-click menu is untouched.
description=
description=[h2]Vehicles and penetration[/h2]
description=[b]15) Vehicle Ballistics & Penetration[/b] (ViewpointVehicleBallistics)
description=Needs ZombieBuddy on [b]server and clients[/b] (the server does the damage). Works with modded vehicles: it uses each vehicle's own physics shapes (also sloped windshields), wheels, seats and the usual part names. All values in Sandbox > Viewpoint Fixes.
description=[list]
description=[*] [b]Real part hit:[/b] vanilla only picks a random part of the side you shoot at and does little damage (200 rifle rounds and the car is still at 30 %). Now the bullet hits what it really touches.
description=[*] [b]Windshield[/b] (laminated glass): keeps its bullet holes and stays in place, caves in after about 15 hits from a 9 mm (rifles count double, adjustable). [b]Side and rear windows[/b] (tempered glass): usually shatter at the first hit. [b]Armoured glass[/b]: scrapes, ricochets.
description=[*] [b]Tyres:[/b] flat after 1 (rifle, slug, magnum), 2 (pistol) or 3 (.22, single pellets) hits - or, by sandbox choice, a slow leak (more holes = faster). Run-flat on armoured vehicles (KI5 RFS/CTIS, wheel armour).
description=[*] [b]Lights:[/b] break at the first hit. [b]Doors, hood, trunk:[/b] damage per hit by calibre. [b]Engine/radiator:[/b] the engine block stops rounds. [b]Fuel tank:[/b] leaks.
description=[*] [b]Armour[/b] (KI5 DAMN...Armor and other mods' armour parts, found by name and position): holds with a scrape and wears down slowly; only very heavy rounds get through.
description=[*] [b]People inside:[/b] rounds through glass or doors can hit occupants (body part by height, bullet wound). Rifles go through doors, small calibres often stay in the sheet metal.
description=[*] [b]Open windows:[/b] shots through a rolled-down window do not touch the glass. Door windows of modded vehicles that cannot be rolled down get the normal Open / Close window option (sandbox).
description=[*] [b]What was hit:[/b] a short text over the shooter, e.g. "Tyre rear left: flat" (server option, players can hide it).
description=[*] [b]Shooting from vehicles:[/b] firearm in hand, hold right mouse to aim, left click to shoot (full auto keeps firing). 3D: where you look, 2D: at the mouse. Your own closed window gets a hole or shatters. Sound, noise, ammo, jams like a normal shot. Driver allowed (sandbox) with more spread.
description=[*] [b]Penetration[/b] (Viewpoint True Ballistics): every round has real energy (mass and velocity of its calibre, mod ammo too, AP stronger, hollow points weaker). Walls, doors, furniture, trees and vehicles need a certain energy per material (all in sandbox, in joules); flat angles need more. The round flies on slower (less damage, more drop) and slightly deflected, up to 3 obstacles. Hard surfaces at flat angles can make it ricochet and fly on.
description=[*] [b]Zombies behind walls can be hit, players not[/b] (default, admin option). The server's line-of-fire check of True Ballistics accepts hits through walls that the round could really pierce.
description=[*] With 2D view only, enable "also in the normal 2D view" (the shot then flies straight along your aim). Without True Ballistics there is no penetration and no shooting from vehicles.
description=[*] Bullet Impacts 2D contains the same vehicle part as a separate mod (VFA_VehicleBallistics2D); if both are enabled this one takes over.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Server settings (Sandbox > Viewpoint Fixes)[/h2]
description=Every mod option of mods 10, 12, 13 and 14 is mirrored on the sandbox page [b]Viewpoint Fixes[/b]. Per mod the admin picks [b]who sets the options[/b]: [i]Players choose[/i] (default, the sandbox values are ignored), [i]Server values (greyed out)[/i] or [i]Server values (hidden)[/i]. With server values everyone uses the sandbox values and the entries in Options > Mods are locked or hidden, so the list stays short. The players' own values stay saved for other games. Key bindings always stay the player's choice. Changes made by an admin during the game apply within one in-game minute.

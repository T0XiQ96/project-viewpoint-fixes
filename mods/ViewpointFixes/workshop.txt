title=Project Viewpoint - Fixes
description=[h1]Project Viewpoint - Fixes[/h1]
description=Fifteen small, independent mods for [b]Project Viewpoint[/b] (3D view), Build 42.21. Enable only the ones you want. All need ZombieBuddy and go after Viewpoint. Mods 1-14 are client-side (12 changes where your own shots go); mod 15 changes gameplay and is needed on the server too. Java source in each mod's source/ folder. Unofficial.
description=
description=[h2]Markers and labels[/h2]
description=[b]1) Viewpoint 2D to 3D[/b] - mods using isoToScreenX/Y (map markers, hints) show at the real 3D spot; Real Vehicle VFX's flat 2D smoke is off in 3D.
description=[b]2) - Extended Patch[/b] - the same for IsoUtils.XToScreen/YToScreen (Leave A Message Note, Shotgun Trajectory, Home Inventory, Cold Breath, Hide Anywhere).
description=
description=[h2]Looks[/h2]
description=[b]3) Open All Containers Fix[/b] - opened containers no longer vanish.
description=[b]4) Hide Camera[/b] - hiding under a bed (Hide Anywhere) drops the camera to the floor.
description=[b]10) 3D Gun Effects[/b] - tracers per ammo type (also True Ballistics rounds), muzzle flash with light, gun smoke at the real muzzle, impacts by material (wood splinters, metal sparks, stone dust, ricochets on hard surfaces), bullet holes, JB's Fireflies in 3D. With mod 15: impacts by the vehicle part hit (glass, metal, tyre, armour) and bullet holes that stay on vehicles and move with them.
description=
description=[h2]Controls[/h2]
description=[b]5) Item Placement 3D[/b] - 3D preview, R / Shift+R rotate, left click places.
description=[b]6) Right Click in Mouse Mode[/b] - right click opens the context menu in mouse mode.
description=[b]7) Crosshair Use[/b] - left click a light switch you look at; short right click = menu for the object in the crosshair.
description=[b]8) Prone on X[/b] - Lethal Stealth prone toggle in 3D.
description=[b]11) Over the Shoulder in 3D[/b] - hold Caps Lock to look around while walking.
description=[b]14) Hold to Zoom[/b] - zoom only while holding a key (needs Viewpoint QOL).
description=
description=[h2]Shooting[/h2]
description=[b]12) Aim Spread[/b] - needs Viewpoint True Ballistics: shots leave inside the game's aiming circle (1.5x), bushes can deflect rounds. Only with "Firearms Use Damage Chance = Disabled".
description=[b]13) Crosshair Options[/b] - centre dot: when, shape, size, colour; aiming brackets on/off.
description=[b]15) Vehicle Ballistics & Penetration[/b] (ViewpointVehicleBallistics) - server and clients:
description=[list]
description=[*] Shots hit the [b]real part[/b] of a vehicle instead of a random part of that side. Works with modded vehicles (their physics shapes, wheels, seats, part names).
description=[*] Windshield (laminated) keeps its holes and caves in after ~15 hits (9 mm; rifles count double). Side/rear windows usually shatter at the first hit. Armoured glass scrapes.
description=[*] Tyres flat after 1 (rifle, slug, magnum), 2 (pistol) or 3 (.22, pellet) hits, or slow leak (sandbox). Run-flat for KI5 RFS/CTIS and wheel armour.
description=[*] Lights break, doors/hood/trunk take damage by calibre, the engine block stops rounds, fuel tanks leak. Armour parts (KI5 and others) hold and wear slowly.
description=[*] Rounds through glass or doors can hit people inside. Rolled-down windows let shots pass; non-openable door windows of modded vehicles become openable (sandbox).
description=[*] The shooter sees what was hit ("Tyre rear left: flat").
description=[*] [b]Shooting from vehicles:[/b] hold right mouse to aim, left click to shoot (3D: view, 2D: mouse). Your own closed window gets a hole or shatters.
description=[*] [b]Penetration[/b] with True Ballistics: real energy per calibre (AP stronger, hollow points weaker); walls, doors, furniture, trees and vehicles need energy per material (sandbox, joules), rounds fly on slower and deflected, ricochets on hard surfaces. Zombies behind walls can be hit, players not (admin option).
description=[*] 2D: enable "also in the normal 2D view". Bullet Impacts 2D has the same vehicle part; with both enabled this one takes over.
description=[/list]
description=

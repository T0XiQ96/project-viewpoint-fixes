description=[b]Full documentation (EN/DE) and source:[/b] [url=https://github.com/T0XiQ96/project-viewpoint-fixes/blob/main/docs/en/BulletImpacts2D.md]GitHub[/url] - Vehicle Ballistics: [url=https://github.com/T0XiQ96/project-viewpoint-fixes/blob/main/docs/en/VehicleBallistics.md]details[/url]
description=
description=Bullet holes, sparks, dust, splinters and ricochets in the normal isometric view of Project Zomboid (Build 42.21).
description=
description=[list]
description=[*] Uses the game's own impact point (where the tracer ends: wall, door, window, vehicle, obstacle).
description=[*] Material from the hit tile, the same one the game uses for the impact sound: wood = dark hole and splinters, metal = small hole and sparks, hardened metal / armoured glass = only a scrape and frequent ricochets, stone / brick / concrete = hole and coloured dust, dirt / grass / sand / snow = spray and a short-lived mark.
description=[*] Ricochets get likelier on hard surfaces at flat angles: sparks and a streak instead of a hole.
description=[*] Size depends on the ammo (rifle, pistol, shotgun pellets; mod ammo recognised by name).
description=[*] Holes on doors and windows vanish when they are opened or broken. Effects get darker in the dark.
description=[*] Options in Options > Mods > Bullet Impacts 2D (holes, lifetime, sparks, dust, ricochets, size).
description=[/list]
description=Needs [b]ZombieBuddy[/b] (a tiny Java hook reads the impact point). Client side: every player who wants the effects needs the mod. With Project Viewpoint's 3D view on, it steps aside; 3D impacts come from "Project Viewpoint - Fixes" (3D Gun Effects).
description=Source in the mod's source/ folder.
description=
description=[h2]Second mod in this item: Vehicle Ballistics 2D (VFA_VehicleBallistics2D)[/h2]
description=Optional, changes gameplay - needed on [b]server and clients[/b] (ZombieBuddy on the server too). Enable it if you want:
description=[list]
description=[*] Shots hit the [b]real part[/b] of a vehicle instead of a random part of that side (vanilla: 200 rifle rounds and the car is still at 30 %). Works with modded vehicles: their physics shapes (also sloped windshields), wheels, seats and part names.
description=[*] Windshield (laminated) keeps its holes and caves in after ~15 hits (9 mm; rifles count double). Side and rear windows usually shatter at the first hit. Armoured glass scrapes.
description=[*] Tyres flat after 1 (rifle, slug, magnum), 2 (pistol) or 3 (.22, single pellet) hits, or slow leak. Run-flat on armoured vehicles (KI5 RFS/CTIS, wheel armour).
description=[*] Lights break, doors, hood and trunk take damage by calibre, the engine block stops rounds, fuel tanks leak. Armour (KI5 and others) holds and wears slowly. Rounds through glass or doors can hit people inside; rolled-down windows let shots pass.
description=[*] The shooter sees what was hit ("Tyre rear left: flat"). Impacts on vehicles use the vehicle's material (glass, metal, rubber, armour) instead of the ground.
description=[*] With Viewpoint True Ballistics also penetration of walls, doors, furniture and trees by material and energy, ricochets, and shooting from vehicles (hold right mouse, left click).
description=[*] All values in Sandbox > Vehicle Ballistics 2D. If "Project Viewpoint - Fixes" (Vehicle Ballistics) is enabled too, that one takes over and this one stays idle.
description=[/list]
